# How to read the records

## Campaign money

The 2026 roster covers statewide executive offices, the U.S. Senate, OH-03/OH-15 and Franklin County auditor/commissioner races, including listed minor-party and write-in candidates. Financial coverage is checked against that same roster. Committee identities, available reports and unresolved matches remain separate; an unmatched candidate is not assigned zero fundraising.

The ledgers cover available 2025–2026 reports. Ohio candidate bulk contributions, expenditures and covers are supplemented with loan schedules. Franklin County scans are transcribed with text recognition, page-image checks and cash-total reconciliation. Federal summaries are read from original electronic covers; the original committee filing indexes catch reports still awaiting summary processing. Current amendments replace superseded reports. Source-file hashes and locators accompany the records.

Totals stay with their committee and reporting period. Contributions, borrowing, transfers, other receipts, refunds and spending have separate categories. A transfer can appear as one committee's payment and another's receipt. Committee totals therefore cannot be added into a unique total of election funding. Balances, outstanding loans, memo items and accounting carry-over subtotals are not new receipts. Federal totals follow the source's accounting basis and may include in-kind entries.

Name groups summarize the wording reported in filings without resolving identities. Linking a donation to a contract or incentive requires separate evidence. Campaign statements, outside spending, charitable gifts, endorsements and candidate receipts retain their own meanings. The available candidate ledgers do not imply exhaustive coverage of outside spending, unfiled activity or later reports.

Each connection is a narrow statement supported by cited material. Its direction, evidence status, relevant period and qualifications belong to that statement. The map's layout and a person's number of connections have no evidentiary meaning.

## Evidence and sources

Reporting, allegations, charges, findings, admissions, convictions, registrations and public authorizations have separate labels. A reporting source remains reporting even when its contents have been checked. Ownership, employment, advisory roles, lobbying and campaign contributions are separate relationships. A path through several records supplies context; it does not establish control, causation, wrongdoing or an exchange.

Source information records content availability, check dates, methods and hashes where available. A successful source check establishes what was retrieved or observed, not the truth of every statement on the page. Verified dated Wayback snapshots are preferred. Otherwise the checked original is cited. Each source has one preferred citation URL.

Initial review attribution is unavailable in this public edition. Source-support check dates are retained where recorded; independent editorial review remains pending. This collection is a starting point for examination, not an independently audited body of findings. Corrections can be proposed against any stable record ID.

## Money and time

Amounts identify their stage and unit: receipts, payments, commitments, authorization ceilings, valuations or penalties. An appropriation is different from money paid. Included transfers and contract modifications have explicit references to related amounts or agreements. Reported contribution aggregates appear once; itemized dates, when available, explain the aggregate.

Event dates, publication dates, archive captures, source checks and relationship validity are separate fields. Unknown days stay unknown. A roster observation is different from the beginning of an engagement. Paths can combine records from different periods; the details identify uncertain or non-overlapping dates.

## Coverage and correction

The collection is selective. Missing connections mean only that no accepted record is included. Research leads remain questions rather than factual edges. Similar names require supporting identity evidence.

Sources and qualifications travel with the JSON/CSV downloads and evidence packets. Cite the record ID and edition so others can locate the same statement. [Suggest a correction](https://github.com/someonelikeTIM/cbus-power-network/issues/new?template=evidence-correction.yml).
